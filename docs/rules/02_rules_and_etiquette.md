# Kizuna Taiwanese Mahjong — Official Rules, Setup & Etiquette

See `00_confirmed_rulings.md` for rulings that settle ambiguities in this file.

## 1. The 144-Tile Set

- **Suit tiles (108 tiles — 4 of each number 1–9):**
  - **Circles (Dots):** represent coins (1 to 9 Circle).
  - **Bamboo (Sticks):** represent strings of coins (1 to 9 Bamboo; 1-Bamboo depicts a bird).
  - **Characters (萬 / Wan):** represent ten-thousand currency (1萬 to 9萬).
- **Honour tiles (28 tiles — 4 of each):**
  - **4 Winds:** East (東), South (南), West (西), North (北).
  - **3 Dragons:** Red Dragon (中), Green Dragon (發), White Dragon (白).
- **Bonus tiles (8 tiles — 1 of each):**
  - **4 Flowers:** Plum (1), Orchid (2), Chrysanthemum (3), Bamboo (4) — indexed in red.
  - **4 Seasons:** Spring (一), Summer (二), Autumn (三), Winter (四) — indexed in blue.
  - *Rule:* bonus tiles are placed face-up immediately upon drawing and replaced from the Flower Wall (back wall).

## 2. Objective & Hand Structure

- **Winning hand formula:** 5 melds (sets) + 1 pair = 17 tiles total.
  - With 1 Gong: 1 set of 4 + 4 sets of 3 + 1 pair of 2 = 18 tiles.
  - With 2 Gongs: 2 sets of 4 + 3 sets of 3 + 1 pair of 2 = 19 tiles.
- **Meld types:**
  - **Sheung (Chow):** 3 consecutive tiles in the same suit (e.g. 3-4-5 Bamboo). Claimed ONLY from the player on your immediate left.
  - **Pong (Pung):** 3 identical tiles (e.g. 中中中). Claimed from any player.
  - **Gong (Kong):** 4 identical tiles. Declaring a Gong requires drawing 1 replacement tile immediately from the Flower Wall so your hand maintains 5 melds + 1 pair.
  - **Promoting an open Pong:** you may add a 4th tile to an exposed Pong only when self-drawn. Another player waiting on that exact tile to win may **Rob the Gong** (+10 pts).

## 3. Discard Claiming Priority

MAHJONG (win) > PONG / GONG > SHEUNG

## 4. Setup, Seating & Dealing

1. **Building the wall:** 4 players shuffle face-down and build a wall 18 tiles long × 2 tiles high in front of each seat, forming a closed square.
2. **Seat assignment:** the last player to arrive rolls 3 dice to determine Temporary East. Place 1 of each Wind tile (E, S, W, N) face-down in the centre. Players draw counterclockwise to take their seats.
3. **Seating & turn order mnemonic:** "Eat Noodle With Soup" — East → North → West → South (counterclockwise play).
4. **Breaking the wall & dealing:** East (dealer) rolls 3 dice, counts stacks from the right end of that wall, and breaks it to form the Flower Wall. Players draw 4 tiles at a time counterclockwise until everyone holds 16 tiles; East takes a 17th tile and discards first. Replace all Flowers/Seasons immediately from the Flower Wall.

## 5. Table Etiquette, Tactical Rules & Penalties

- **Sideways winning tile rule:** when declaring Mahjong, place the winning tile sideways next to/on top of the set it completes *before* opening your hand. Racking the tile into your concealed hand or revealing incorrectly triggers a False Mahjong.
- **Concealed vs. exposed hands:**
  - **Concealed hand:** all sets are drawn by yourself (no discards claimed). Scores higher on applicable patterns.
  - **Exposed (not concealed) hand:** at least one set was claimed from another player's discard.
- **Dead hand (short / long hand):** having too many or too few tiles (e.g. forgetting a Flower/Gong replacement draw) renders the hand dead. The player cannot declare Mahjong or claim sets/flowers/gongs, must continue picking and discarding, and pays no 25-point penalty.
- **False Mahjong penalty:** declaring Mahjong with invalid sets, a missing pair, or fewer points than the round minimum costs 25 points paid to each of the other 3 players. The round is void and the dealer remains unchanged.
- **Missed discard rule:** once a discard is passed (not claimed for Pong/Mahjong) and the next player draws/racks, you cannot claim that same tile if discarded again in the same turn round.
- **Falsely called discard:** if a player misnames a discard (e.g. calls "1 Circle" while throwing "1 Bamboo"), any player waiting on either tile may call Mahjong on it.
- **Shared win (simultaneous Mahjong):** if two players call Mahjong on the exact same discard, both win (shared win) and East rotates counterclockwise.
- **Chasing tiles (4th discard penalty):** when all 4 copies of a tile are discarded in a round, the initial player who threw the 4th tile pays each player 1 Base (5 pts) for Winds/Dragons or 2 Base (10 pts) for suit tiles. (Variant card note: 3/5 pts.)
- **The 4th West Wind exception:** discarding all 4 West Winds in the same round is inauspicious. The player holding the 4th West Wind must show it, keep it, pay 5 points to each player, and discard a different tile that turn (discards West Wind next turn).
- **Drawn game (no Mahjong):** if no one wins before the wall ends, East passes and each player adds 1 Base (5 pts) (or 3 pts per tournament card) into the centre pot for the next self-draw winner.
