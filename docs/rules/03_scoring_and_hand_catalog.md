# Kizuna Taiwanese Mahjong — Complete Scoring & Hand Catalog

See `00_confirmed_rulings.md` for rulings that settle two-value entries in this file.

## 1. Minimum Points Required Per Round

- **East round:** 20 points
- **South round:** 22 points
- **West round:** 24 points
- **North round:** 26 points

## 2. Dealer Streak ("Dice on the Wall")

When East wins consecutively, East places a die on the wall to track streak wins (1, 2, 3). East's required minimum score drops while other players still need the full round standard:

| Round | Standard | 1st streak win | 2nd streak win | 3rd streak win |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **East round** | 20 pts | 17 pts | 15 pts | 13 pts |
| **South round** | 22 pts | 19 pts | 17 pts | 15 pts |
| **West round** | 24 pts | 21 pts | 19 pts | 17 pts |
| **North round** | 26 pts | 23 pts | 21 pts | 19 pts |

- **Streak bonus payout:** East collects extra points from each player (1 = +3 pts, 2 = +5 pts, 3 = +7 pts; 4th streak = +9 pts). Forgetting to place the die forfeits the streak bonus for that round.

## 3. Base Points, Modifiers & Special Bonuses

- **Mahjong base:** 5 pts
- **Good Eye pair (2, 5 or 8):** 2 pts
- **Self-pick (self-draw):** +2 pts (or +3 pts tournament card)
- **Self-pick from Flower Wall:** +5 pts (or +10 pts tournament card)
- **True single wait / calling by pairs:** +2 pts (waiting on 1 exact tile; not valid in Nico Nico or Orphans)
- **No Flowers & no Honours bonus:** +5 pts (stacks with Two-Suit, Pure Suit, Simple/No-Terminal, or Terminal hands; exception: All Sheung with no flowers/honours flat-scores 15 pts)
- **Flower set bonuses:** matching seat flower = +1 pt; Mixed Bouquet (1–4 mixed) = +3 pts; Pure Bouquet (all 4 Flowers or all 4 Seasons) = +5 pts
- **7 Flowers instant win:** flat 20 pts (or round minimum: 20/22/24/26) from each player
- **8 Flowers instant win:** flat 40 pts from each player
- **Fully concealed hand / fully concealed self-draw:** 10 pts / 15 pts
- **Last Man Standing (fully exposed):** 10 pts (5 pts if closing on pair)
- **Robbing a Gong to Mahjong:** +10 pts

## 4. Suit & Basic Hands

- **All Sheung hand (10 pts):** 5 Sheungs + 1 pair.
- **All Pong hand (25 pts):** 5 Pongs + 1 pair.
- **Two Suit hand (10 pts):** only 2 numbered suits, no Honours.
- **Semi-Pure Suit hand (30 pts):** 1 numbered suit combined with Honours.
- **Pure Suit hand (90 pts):** all tiles in 1 single numbered suit, no Honours.
- **Five Suit hand (10 pts):** all 3 numbered suits + Dragons + Winds.
- **Simple hand — no Terminals & no Honours (10 pts):** middle numbers 2–8 only (no 1s, 9s or Honours).

## 5. Terminal Hands

- **Pure Terminal Sheungs (5 pts):** 1-2-3 + 7-8-9 sequences in the same suit.
- **Double Terminal Sheung — Mixed (20 pts):** 1-2-3 + 7-8-9 in suit A, plus 1-2-3 + 7-8-9 in suit B.
- **Double Terminal Sheung — Pure (35 pts):** two sets of 1-2-3 + two sets of 7-8-9 all in the SAME suit.
- **Pure Terminal Pongs (5 pts):** Pongs of 111 + 999 in the same suit.
- **Terminal Pong Mix — 2 suits (20 pts):** 4 Pongs of 111 and/or 999 across 2 suits.
- **Terminal Only hand with Honours (20 pts):** all terminal sets (123, 789, 111, 999) + Honours (numbered pair must be 1s or 9s).
- **Terminal Only hand — no Honours (40 pts):** all terminal sets only, no Honours (pair must be 1s or 9s).

## 6. Sequence & Sheung Patterns (*varies if concealed)

- **Mixed Dragon (8 pts exposed / 10 pts concealed*):** 1-2-3, 4-5-6 and 7-8-9 across all 3 suits.
- **Pure Dragon (15 pts exposed / 20 pts concealed*):** 1-2-3, 4-5-6 and 7-8-9 all in 1 suit.
- **Sisters (identical Sheungs in different suits):** 2 sets = 3 pts; 3 sets (all 3 suits) = 10 pts (15 pts if concealed*).
- **Brothers (identical Sheungs in same suit):** 2 sets = 5 pts; 3 sets = 30 pts; 4 sets = 40 pts (or 50 pts).
- **Sister-Brother Combination Sheung hand (35 pts):** combines Sister runs and Brother runs in one hand (no individual sister/brother points added).
- **Step-Up hand (10 pts exposed / 15 pts concealed*):** 3 Sheungs across all 3 suits stepping up by +1 (e.g. 2-3-4 萬, 3-4-5 條, 4-5-6 筒).
- **All Step-Up hand (30 pts):** 5 Sheungs stepping up by +1 across 1, 2 or 3 suits (includes All Sheung).
- **Gong in 2 / 3 / 4 ways:** 2 Gongs = 5 pts; 3 Gongs = 15 pts; 4 Gongs = 30 pts.

## 7. Pong, Wind & Dragon Hands

- **Sister Pongs (same number in different suits):** 2 sets = 5 pts; 3 sets = 30 pts.
- **Uncle Pongs (consecutive Pongs in same suit):** 2 sets = 5 pts; 3 sets = 30 pts; 4 sets = 50 pts (or 40); 5 sets = 60 pts; 111-222-333-444-555 + 66 Mahjong = 80 pts.
- **Concealed Pongs bonus:** 2 concealed Pongs = 5 pts; 3 = 15 pts; 4 = 40 pts; 5 = 80 pts.
- **Winds:** Pong of seat/round Wind = 1 pt each; **Little Three Winds** (2 Pongs + 1 pair) = 15 pts; **Big Three Winds** (3 Pongs) = 30 pts; **Little Four Winds** (3 Pongs + 1 pair) = 60 pts; **Big Four Winds** (4 Pongs) = 80 pts.
- **Dragons:** Pong of any Dragon = 2 pts; **Little Dragons** (2 Pongs + 1 pair) = 20 pts; **Big Dragons** (3 Pongs) = 40 pts.

## 8. Special Hands & Chicken Hand

- **Nico Nico (7 pairs + 1 Pong):**
  - Winning tile completes the **Pong**: 50 pts.
  - Winning tile completes the **final pair**: 60 pts.
  - With **1 undeclared Gong** in hand (counts as 2 pairs): 70 pts.
  - With **2 undeclared Gongs** in hand (counts as 4 pairs): 80 pts.
- **Orphans:**
  - **Standard Orphans (70 pts):** unrelated suit tiles (minimum 3 numbers apart) + all 7 Honours + any pair.
  - **Orphans with 1s & 9s (80 pts):** unrelated suit tiles including 1s & 9s in all 3 suits + all 7 Honours + any pair.
- **Gem hands (20 pts + Semi-Pure 30 pts = 50 pts total):**
  - **Jade hand (20 pts):** Pong of Green Dragons + all other tiles Bamboo.
  - **Ruby hand (20 pts):** Pong of Red Dragons + all other tiles Characters.
  - **Diamond hand (20 pts):** Pong of White Dragons + all other tiles Circles.
- **Pure Honour hand (140 pts):** 5 Pongs of Honours + 1 pair of Honours.
- **Chicken hand (雞胡) (20 pts):** a winning hand scoring exactly 2 pattern points exclusive of base and flowers (e.g. 1 Dragon Pong = 2 pts, OR 1 Good Eye = 2 pts, OR 2 exposed non-seat/non-round Wind Pongs = 2 pts) with zero other scoring elements.
